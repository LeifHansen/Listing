---
title: Etsy tells you before it turns you away
date: 2026-09-22
---

Etsy only lets approved shops connect, and it enforces that on its own page
after you leave the app — so a seller could press Connect and land on
"Only the app owner may authorize a seller app" with no way to tell whether
something here was broken. The Etsy card now says what Etsy is about to
decide, and confirms before handing your browser over. You can still go
through; you just aren't sent there blind.

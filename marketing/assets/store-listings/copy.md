## Full description for both stores

```text
Turn your thrift finds into eBay listings with Thryft Shop. Start with a few photos, get an AI-written draft, and review it before you publish. Built for resellers who would rather spend their time sourcing than filling out listing forms.

START WITH PHOTOS
Take photos or add them from your library. Thryft Shop drafts the title, description and item specifics, then suggests a category and price. Edit the details and publish when you’re ready.

DRAFT A WHOLE HAUL
Upload photos of multiple items in a batch. Review how the photos were grouped, add details about each item, and create a separate draft for each find. Use optional background removal to clean up product shots.

CHECK A FIND IN SHOP MODE
Scan an item while you’re sourcing to identify it and see a resale estimate when comparable listings are available. Scan a shelf or rack to flag finds worth a closer look.

MANAGE YOUR EBAY LISTINGS
Connect your eBay seller account to publish and manage live listings. Adjust a price, send eligible offers to watchers, or end and relist an item. Start a draft on your phone and pick it up on the web using the same Thryft Shop account.

A Thryft Shop account is required to save drafts. Publishing and live listing management require a connected eBay seller account. AI-generated details and price estimates should be reviewed before publishing.

eBay is a trademark of eBay Inc. Thryft Shop is independently operated and is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay.
```
